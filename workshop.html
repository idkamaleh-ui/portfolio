Exhibition Build · eBarza at INDEX Dubai

Designed, fabricated in the partner workshop and installed on site: the eBarza stand at the INDEX design exhibition in Dubai. Timber pallet shelving on galvanised steel frames, mesh-clad display towers and a suspended mesh sign box create an industrial showroom for eBarza's furniture and lighting, alongside a branded Pixel Interior feature wall.

Kiosk Build · Tulip Flowers, Dubai

A flower kiosk for Tulip Flowers, fabricated in the partner workshop and assembled on site in a Dubai mall. Welded black steel frames with cross-bracing carry OSB panel walls, open display shelving and hanging vase rails, finished with a timber deck, an OSB-clad service counter, a branded sign box and Edison filament pendants.

Office Fit-Out · Fujairah Port Offices

A full office refurbishment for Fujairah Port, delivered from strip-out to handover in partnership with Pixel Studio. The existing ceilings, lighting and partitions were removed, and the space was rebuilt with a linear slatted ceiling and integrated light lines, timber slat and veneer wall panelling, marble-clad feature walls and built-in joinery made in the partner workshop.
